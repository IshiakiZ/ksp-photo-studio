Photo Studio @VERSION@
for Kerbal Space Program 1.12.x

Your craft in a photographer's studio: a white cyclorama, a green screen or a showroom with a polished floor. A camera that goes round it, a turntable, and pictures larger than the screen.

INSTALL
  Copy both folders in this download's GameData (Keystone and PhotoStudio) into your KSP's
  GameData folder, and start the game. Keystone is the base this mod stands on; one copy
  serves every mod that uses it. To remove the mod, delete the PhotoStudio folder.

USE
  In the assembly building, the hangar, or in flight, press Option-P on a Mac (Alt-P
  elsewhere), or open the Keystone window (the keystone button on the toolbar, or Option-K /
  Alt-K), turn to Photo Studio and press "Open the studio". Right mouse button: turn round
  the craft. Wheel: nearer and further. Middle button: raise or lower the view.
  Option-Return (Alt-Return) takes a picture, into the game's Screenshots folder.
  Option-P again, or Escape, closes the studio and puts everything back as it was.
  In flight the game is paused while the studio is open.

WHERE IT WORKS
  Made and tried on a Mac. It uses only the game's own shaders, so nothing in it is meant
  for one system only, but nobody has run it on Windows or Linux yet.
